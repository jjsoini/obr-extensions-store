# Filters & Effects

- **Viewport filters** — full-screen visual filters (night, horror, underwater, CRT…) that are applied to the entire viewport.
- **Item effects** — animated effects on tokens and items (fire, smoke, rays, lightning, auras, glitches, warps…).

## Viewport filters

Click the **Filters & Effects** button in the toolbar to apply filters to the entire viewport - selected filters do not automatically show to others until you sync them into the scene.

- **Your screen** — preview filters and apply them to your screen only. Changing the filter does not affect what others see.
- **Scene** — shows the filter that is applied to everyone viewing the scene. Filter applied to a scene is loaded automatically when opening the scene.
- **Sync to scene** (GM) — Updates your current filter into the scene and everyone viewing it.

Filters are applied locally. Users and GM can always turn off the filter on their own screen or select another one.

## Item effects

Add effects from the context menu ('Add Effect'). You can tweak each effect in the settings panel.

Some effects are not fully compatible with other effects or viewport filters, see below.

**Compatibility:** Effects that distort the image are not compatible with viewport filters or overlapping other distortion effects. These are marked with an asterisk in the effect menu.

**Compatibility mode** switch: turn it on to use a simpler effect that is compatible with other effects and viewport filters.

## Other features

### Text autotyper (GM)

In the toolbar popover, **Text autotyper** animates the selected text items character by character, pausing at spaces and paragraph breaks.
