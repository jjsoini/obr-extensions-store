# Dice

Beautiful 3D dice extension for Daggerheart

**Duality Dice**

Roll the Hope and Fear dice with one button. Add advantage or disadvantage and a modifier, and the result shows whether you rolled with Hope, with Fear or a Critical Success.

**Damage and other rolls**

Roll any mix of d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20 in Hope or Fear colours.

**Shared rolls**

Everyone sees your rolls in real time. Rolls can also be hidden.

**Phones and tablets**

On a phone or a small tablet the tray fills the screen, with big dice buttons in a panel over it.
